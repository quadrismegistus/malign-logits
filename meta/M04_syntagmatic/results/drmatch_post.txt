## 1. WHY NOT A SPEECH-VERB FILTER

v4's own flags: faller `is_speech` 15.5% against matched 7.5%. But the MEDIAN dialogue rate is identical across the arms (5.3% vs 5.2%) -- the imbalance is entirely upper-tail. A class filter removes the wrong words and keeps the right ones.

## 2. THE PROPOSAL, AND THE TOLERANCE IS THE ONLY FREE PARAMETER

`matched` is currently the non-mover minimising |log2(Q/Q_faller)|. I make probability a HARD GATE and dialogue rate the objective inside it:

    candidates unchanged (non-movers, IS_VV, != faller, post > 0)
    keep those with |log2(Q_w / Q_faller)| <= TOL
    among them choose min |rate(w) - rate(faller)|
    if none qualify, fall back to the existing rule

Ordering matters: gating on dialogue and optimising probability would let the probability match drift without bound. **TOL set by a rule declared before I read the sweep**: the spec's own match bar is a factor of 1.4, and the median must stay at least 3x inside it. **My first guess was 0.5 and the sweep rejected it** (median 0.285, only 1.8x inside).
