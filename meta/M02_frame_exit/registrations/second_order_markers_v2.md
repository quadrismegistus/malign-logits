## WHAT CHANGED, AND ON WHOSE EVIDENCE

**DROPPED: `at the same time`, `simultaneous*`.** Agents 05, 06 and 07
independently judged the simultaneity adverbial to be INSIDE the state:
"the adverbial modifies the two acts; nothing nominalises the state that makes
them simultaneous" (07); "flags simultaneity, but the predicates are still
simply asserted, not characterised" (06). Agents 04 and 08 disagreed and listed
it as a marker. The tie is broken by evidence that predates the harvest: it is
v1's weakest marker at 1.71x alone, and leave-one-out RAISES the contrast from
2.37x to 3.03x when it is removed. Qualitative judgement and the dilution
signature agree.

**ADDED: pair-deixis, 8 of 10 agents, most naming it their pattern 1.** A
pro-form or quantifier that takes the two poles as one referent and then
governs a verb -- `be both`, `be neither of them`, `take a chance on either`,
`be the latter`, `hate both sides`, `use both`, `warts and all`.

**AND THE TRAP IN IT, from agent 08's edge case rather than its phrase list:**
"He was both tortured and punished" -- *"the `both` is real but conjoins
near-synonyms, not the loyal/rebellious pair."* Bare `both` is among the
commonest words in English. What the agents described is a CONSTRUCTION, not a
word, so `both` enters only in pronominal position after a governing verb, with
a negative lookahead excluding the correlative `both X and Y`. If that guard is
wrong the pole arm will show it.

**ADDED: condition-nouns beyond v1's, 10 of 10 agents.** `dilemma`,
`quandary`, `dualit*`, `turmoil`, `push-and-pull`, `mutually exclusive`,
`double-edged`, and the mixture heads `a mix/mixture/blend/swirl/tangle/
combination of`.

**ADDED: container and gap figures, 9 of 10.** `trapped in`, `the space
between`, `bridge the gap`, `barrier between`.

**ADDED: reflexive self-division, 3 of 10.** `contradicting himself`,
`lost herself`, `separated from her body`.

**REJECTED though convergent: bare `it`-deixis** (agents 02, 09). Correct as
analysis -- a bare pronoun can carry the abstraction -- and unusable as an
instrument, since it would fire on nearly every passage.

**REJECTED though convergent: oxymoronic compound NP** (8 of 10) -- `his
captive freedom`, `lovely monster`, `happy lying innocence`. The strongest
qualitative pattern in the harvest and not expressible as a regex without an
antonym-pair resource. Recorded as owed work, not silently dropped.
