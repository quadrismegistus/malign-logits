## §1 THE CLAIM IT DEFENDS, AND THE ONE-LINE ATTACK ON IT

**Registration C: valence de-extremification CONFIRMED, on the frozen 959.**

**The attack a hostile reader reaches in one line:** the 959 is **39%
transgressive by domain** (violence 221, sexual 89, plus profanity, substance,
death, power), 41% cross-lingual, 25% neutral. **A claim stated generally, tested
on a corpus more than a third transgressive.** If the confirmed A is carried by
that fraction, the general form of the claim is not supported and nobody has
checked.

**C cannot answer it** — the contamination is inside C's own population.

---

## §2 THE POPULATION, AND WHY IT IS THE RIGHT ONE

**The 684 UNMARKED pair members.** They are the cleanest neutral corpus the
campaign owns, and the reason is construction rather than sampling:

    MARKED    She stirred the crushed sedative into his coffee and
    UNMARKED  She stirred the crushed cinnamon into his coffee and

**Ordinary sentences, matched to a transgressive twin on syntax, length, topic,
register and slot position, with the transgressive element removed.** Better than
the 959 (39% transgressive) and better than found prose (one high-craft register,
23.9% cell yield).

**MEASURED BEFORE THIS WAS WRITTEN, not assumed:**

    UNMARKED  684 members   635 with >=1 qualifying cell   92.8%
              cells per member  median 31   q1 26   q3 33
    (MARKED yields identically: 635, median 31 -- the construction did not
     thin one side)

**Against the literary corpus's 23.9%. The instrument works here.**

## §3 THE QUANTITY, NAMED AS AN EXPRESSION

    A_|valence| on each UNMARKED member, computed by C's OWN estimator --
    `C3.A_and_terms(vals, ws, rs)` -> wmean(FALLERS) - wmean(RISERS),
    weights |delta|, uncentred -- with C's residualisation form, NOT D2's.

**THIS IS NOT D2's STORED `A_unmarked`.** D2's `val_extrem` arm residualises
QUADRATICALLY on arousal; C's H2 residualises LINEARLY. **Comparing D2's stored
number against C's benchmark would compare two estimators and call the difference
a population effect.** The quantity is recomputed in C's form.

**THE BENCHMARK, and BOTH forms are reported because one of them has to be
wrong:**

    b INHERITED FROM C    the slope C fitted on the 959.  Fewer things vary --
                          but it was fitted on a 39% transgressive population
                          and may not transfer, which is the very thing under
                          test.
    b RE-FITTED on the    appropriate to the population, but a SECOND changed
      unmarked members    quantity, so a difference could be either.

**Neither is safe alone. Both print, and the reading rule (§4) requires them to
agree.**

**AGGREGATION:** per member first, then over the 635 — matching D2's unit, not a
cell-pooled figure.

---

## §4 THE READING RULE, FIXED BEFORE ANY NUMBER, ALL OUTCOMES INFORMATIVE

    A beats the benchmark at C's level, both b forms agreeing
      -> C's claim SURVIVES on a clean neutral population.  Composition was
         not carrying it, and the one-line attack is answered.

    A beats it BY LESS
      -> C's number is PARTLY composition.  Report the discount; C's claim is
         scoped from "in general" to "and by this much on neutral prompts."

    A does not beat it
      -> C's general claim DOES NOT HOLD on neutral language, and must be
         scoped to its own corpus in the draft.  THE MOST USEFUL OUTCOME and
         the reason to run this before August rather than after.

    the two b forms DISAGREE
      -> the slope does not transfer, which is itself a finding about the 959,
         and neither reading is taken.

## §5 EXPOSURE, DECLARED RATHER THAN DEFENDED

**RH's decision: this is EXPLORATORY, reported as such, and the label is
accurate.** `A_unmarked` was not fished for — **§D1 REQUIRES both terms to print
beside every D**, so it fell out of a pre-registered design as a byproduct.

**WHAT IS KNOWN, AND BY WHOM:** D2's stored `A_unmarked` (−0.0323, quadratic
form) is public and this seat has quoted it repeatedly. **The quantity here — C's
LINEAR form on the unmarked members — has never been computed at any seat.** The
related value and its sign are known; this one is not.

**THE CONSEQUENCE, STATED PLAINLY:** the reading rule above is fixed in these
bytes before the run, but it is written by a seat that knows the neighbouring
number. **It is exploratory, it is labelled exploratory, and no confirmatory
weight attaches to it.**

## §6 WHAT WOULD MAKE THIS THE WRONG MEASUREMENT

    1. IN OUR FAVOUR: the unmarked members are MATCHED TWINS, not a sample of
       neutral language.  If the pair FRAME carries pragmatic connotation --
       "crushed cinnamon in his coffee" still cueing poisoning -- then A here
       is elevated and C's claim looks better supported than it is.  NOT
       CORRECTED, DECLARED.

    2. A ROLE CONTRAST HAS NO NATURAL ZERO, which is why the benchmark exists
       and why the raw level is not reported as a finding on its own.

    3. ONE CONSTRUCTION.  684 sentences built by this project for one purpose.
       A survival here is survival on constructed neutral prose, not on
       language.
