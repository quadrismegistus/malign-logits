# M01 — the registrations, by letter

**The record: what exists, what it says, whether it ran, what came back.** No interpretation — which question each registration answers, and how they fit together, is the README's job. Caveats, supersessions and what a number may not say are `ledger.md`'s.

**Why this file has to exist.** A registration is frozen before its numbers exist and locked at the freeze. **The run happens afterwards, so the document can never record its own result.** That is why every registration below except N and O still says "draft; not in force as declared" in its own status line, including six that have run with artifacts on disk. It is not neglect: **a locked file cannot update itself, so the run state can only live outside it.** This is that outside.

Letters run B–G then L–S. **There is no A, H, I, J or K** — the letters were never assigned, not retired. **S is the last lettered registration: RH ended pre-registration for this programme on 2026-08-06 (docket [4712]); what follows is the reproducible-vs-not regime — held-back samples, free analysis, replication as the control — and this table closes with S.**

## P, Q and R in detail, because one-word states hide different things

**P — RAN 2026-08-04.** Six firings on RH's explicit word (~$35), full coverage: 4,443 items × 3 coder families, zero missing. **The relation has its first positive characterisation:** in ACT slots all three families independently read the risen word as an exclamation more often than the stationary decoy from the same faller — a speech-act shift, the act rendered as a cry. The metonymy reading (REF stratum's declared primary) did not survive: one family of three. Two standing qualifiers travel with every P sentence: inter-coder agreement is LOW (the alphas print before the verdicts), and 26.7% of identical re-calls differ, so the artifact is one draw from a distribution over runs. **A limit found after the run ([4581]/[4590]): P licenses NO paradigmatic-vs-syntagmatic sentence** — the axis was never a coder-facing question but a post-hoc bucketing of labels, and it splits items all coders call related 49.7%, driven by label tick-rate (1.30–1.85 labels/annotation across coders), not perception. That limit is what R exists to fix.

**Q — FROZEN AND RAN 2026-08-05.** Frozen at `c28ec53f2fe0276a` after sixteen revisions from the reviewed draft; ran logit-local (no spend); every gate fired. **The bridge does not hold, and the registration had pre-written the sentence that fired:** the pair frame and the general corpus disagree about the *direction* of substitution at transgressive content — more-negative at the marked twin inside pairs, more-positive against neutral prompts in the corpus residue. Reported as a frame reversal, never as an asymmetry. Two errata: §Q6's known answer names no arm (Erratum 1, [4495]); H2's branch sentence carried a verdict word for a direction Q never registered (Erratum 2, [4570] — a pair-frame line read as governing a corpus contrast; the corrected artifact is verified and awaits co-signature).

**R — PILOT RUN, CONFIRMATORY PREPARED AND COUNTERSIGNED, HELD ON RH'S FIRE.** The redesigned schema asks directly what P could only derive: the axis as its own field, ONE relation label per coding (the tick-rate leak dies at source), BOTH/NEITHER explicit. A 50-stem pilot (8 coders, 1,600 annotations, exposure declared on the docket before any number existed) found the axis result in its narrowed two-leg form — at transgressive sites the promoted word is contentful and relates as CO_ACT where a matched near-miss non-mover is neither; at neutral sites no difference — and survived a frequency control, four near-independent selection controls, a pool diagnostic and two attacks that died on their own checks. The confirmatory registration (H1 displacement, H2 substitute formation; separate verdicts; argmax decoy kept as the harder control; registered nulls with stated MDEs) is prepared on 254 unspent stems, custody-complete at commit `f2bac1c0`, countersigned, and fires only on RH's explicit word.
