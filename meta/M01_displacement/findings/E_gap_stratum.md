# Findings E: the flattening replicates on a blind stratum, lineage by lineage

Split out of `C_to_O_registered_letters.md` on 2026-08-12 (RH's commission), rewritten the same day to be readable on its own: the hypotheses are stated in plain terms rather than by registration number. `REGISTRATIONS.md` remains authoritative for every number; the registration files hold the frozen statistical detail.

## What was asked

C's de-extremification result (emotionally extreme words fall, neutral words rise, corpus-wide) needed a replication with teeth: on a stratum of the data that had been held blind, with the model LINEAGE as the unit — so that one family of models with many variants cannot carry the result by itself.

## What was found

**Nineteen of twenty-five lineages show the flattening, p 0.0073.** Counting each base-to-aligned lineage once, the de-extremification direction holds in 19 of 25 — the corpus-wide result is not an artifact of a few heavily sampled families.
