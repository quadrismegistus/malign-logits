## §1 WHY, AND THE CORPUS ARGUMENT STANDS ALONE

**Every deep-passage corpus this campaign holds sits on narrow content.** Y is five sexual prompts sampled deep; M02's L2 is the contradiction battery. Everything domain-general is **ten-token beams** (`beam_fc`), and beam text is the mode rather than a sample — so mean surprisal does not estimate entropy on it, and every entropy, KL and predictability instrument built this week is unusable there. That is the gap.

It also settles M04/A's open confound, written into A's own spec §8 before its run:

> The faller is by construction low-probability **under aligned** […] conditioning on it places the aligned model in a state it already assigns low probability to; the next token inherits that mechanically. **Separating them requires a word matched on improbability-under-aligned but NOT demoted by alignment. No collected corpus has one.**

One now does — `data/forced_arms_46reps.json`, built by `scripts/build_forced_arms_105.py` (registrar, commits `96c759b5` and `7b2e3a12`). **It has never been generated.** `beam_fc` holds `undisturbed` 16,786 / `force_faller` 15,348 / `force_riser` 15,264 and no matched arm.

**Sampled, not beams**, because mean surprisal estimates entropy only on samples. **256 tokens** because the predictability effect is marginal at 10 (24 of 33 pairs, p=0.065) and p=3e-05 at 256. **Cross-scoring is not an open cost item** — L2 already scored every sequence under both arms; the "1 in 10" I twice reported at [5245]/[5246] was a ratio of row counts at two different grains, corrected at [5248] by opening a score file.

**On `n_samples` — MEASURED, not chosen, and my original reasoning was backwards.** [5450] argued N buys precision on the per-site mean "not degrees of freedom", implying N is the cheap axis. lacan measured it (`meta/M04_syntagmatic/scripts/passage_variance_components.py`, full corpora):

    within-site sd of mean surprisal   0.61 nats/token   (f11_l2, 22,439 cells)
                                       0.57              (y,       4,488 cells)
    between-site sd of per-site mean   0.35              (115 pairs)
    ratio sd_w/sd_b                    3.1x  ->  variance ratio ~9.6

**The per-site mean is the noisy layer and N is what fixes it.** Two independent corpora, different domains, 7% apart — replication a variance estimate rarely gets.

    Var(pair statistic) ~ (sd_b^2 + sd_w^2/N) / S,  S ~ 178 sites

    N       above the N=inf floor        cost
    5             27%                    $26
    10            14%                    $52
    20             8%                   $104

**There is a floor at sd_b that no N crosses.** N=10 sits 14% above it; N=5 sits 27% above it for half the money.

## §6 WHAT IT ANSWERS, AND WHAT IT CANNOT

**Answers:** whether A's post-utterance shock is about **DEMOTION** or about **any word the aligned model finds improbable**. `FALLER` against `FALLER-MATCHED` holds aligned probability fixed (median |log2 ratio| 0.056; 87.1% within a factor of 1.4) and varies only movement history. A cannot currently tell these apart and says so.

**Cannot answer:** anything requiring the base arm's probability to be held. The match is on the POST arm by design — the confound is what the ALIGNED model finds improbable — so the matched word has a different base probability than the faller. That is the right control for "the aligned model is in a low-probability state" and the wrong one for "this word was demoted FROM somewhere high."

**Cannot answer, NAMED INDIVIDUALLY — because "site-grain" is a label that swallows the distinction that matters.** Every one of A's analyses runs through `per_pair`: median over ~178 sites, then Wilcoxon over pairs. **None is a single-site statement.** What differs is the noise of the per-site INPUT, not the aggregation structure:

    A's PRIMARY delta      mean_lp = mean over beams AND positions    IN
    A's FOUR TERMS         mean_lp, identically                       IN
    A's POSITION PROFILE   mean_at(i) = ONE TOKEN per site            PENDING

**IN, and cleaner than A currently has it: the four-term decomposition.** At 256 tokens each per-site value is a mean over 256 positions rather than 10 — roughly 5x less noisy per site than A's own version. **A's most robust result lives here** (`A|A` against `A|B`, the self-relation asymmetry that survived when the scorer-split framing did not). An earlier draft of this section excluded it by applying an SE for a SINGLE-SITE claim to an analysis that never makes one; that was a coarse predicate at the wrong grain and it would have discarded the best-conditioned thing in the run ([5457]).

**IN AT N=20, MARGINAL AT N=10, OUT AT N=5: the position/per-index profile — and the measurement inverted the worry that produced this paragraph.** `mean_at(i)` takes one token per site, and single-token sd is indeed far larger — **2.70 nats against 0.61 for the 256-token mean, 4.4x, stable across two corpora** ([5459]). But its effect is 3.9x bigger (0.04066 against 0.0104), so **it comes out ahead**:

    study SE = sqrt((sd_b^2 + sd_w^2/N)/S) / sqrt(P),  S=178 sites, P=46 pairs

                        sd_w   sd_b    effect      N=5     N=10    N=20    N=50
    FOUR TERMS          0.61   0.35    0.0104     2.12     2.36    2.51    2.62
    POSITION +1         2.70   0.89    0.04066    2.33     2.77    3.10    3.38
                                                                    ^ clears ~2.8

**So the fork favours BUYING depth rather than declaring the analysis dead**, which is the opposite of what my [5457] expected. Position +1 is also the QUIETEST token on both corpora, not the noisiest.

**And the four-term decomposition is EFFECT-limited, not sample-limited.** Its ceiling is `sd_b`, so more N does not rescue it — it goes 2.12 → 2.62 across a tenfold increase in samples and never clears a conventional bar. It is IN because it is worth having at ~5x cleaner per site than A's own version, not because this run makes it decisive.

**The model is validated against A itself:** A's primary CI [−0.01852, −0.00084] over 33 pairs implies a study SE of 0.00451; the model predicts 0.00441 at N=10 over 46 pairs. **Agreement to 2% is why the table is quotable rather than arithmetic on assumptions.**

**THE ASSUMPTION UNDER THE WHOLE TABLE, unmeasured** (lacan [5459].5): every effect size above is A's, measured on **10-token beams**, applied to **256-token sampled passages**. If the phenomenon is weaker at passage length the table shifts and the fork moves. It cannot be closed from data we hold, and it is the same assumption §4's 256-token choice already rests on.

**IF THE PROFILE IS OUT, THIS RUN EXTENDS A RATHER THAN REPLACING ITS INSTRUMENT, AND SAYS SO.** A's sharpest claim — the +1 spike, gone by +2, "a single-token event" — was made at ten tokens on beams and stays A's claim on A's substrate. Nothing here licenses reading the passage corpus as a like-for-like upgrade of it.

**THE ONE UNMEASURED BOUND, which softens the paragraph above and nothing else** (lacan [5453], verbatim): A's Δ is a DOUBLE difference and its four terms are correlated within a site; correlation cancels variance, and neither corpus carries paired forced arms, so the correlation cannot be estimated from what we hold. **The site-grain numbers are an UPPER bound on the noise.** The pair-level table is unaffected, resting on the two spreads directly.

**Cannot answer:** anything about the 860 cells (10.5%) with no qualifying non-mover. Match quality is a COLUMN (`log2_ratio`), never a gate — the producer's rule, and the reason the campaign's exhibit cell survives at all. **Any analysis conditioning on match quality must declare its threshold before seeing the outcome.**
