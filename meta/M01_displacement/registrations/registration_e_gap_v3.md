## §E0b WHAT §C8 BOUND, AND WHAT IT DID NOT

Registration C's §C8 declared the gap **printed, never tested**, before any data
existed. **It bound as to TESTING — H2's statistic was not computed on the gap
under C, and malign stopped at that line at [1807] with outcome-blind counts on
one side and the effect on the other.**

**IT DID NOT BIND AS TO QUOTING.** The print was read, and the reading became a
qualifier in circulation — *"a property of movement, not of displacement
sites"* — which has been withdrawn on exactly that ground. **Ledger: A CLAUSE
THAT FORBIDS TESTING A QUANTITY MUST ALSO FORBID QUOTING IT, OR THE PRINT
BECOMES THE HEDGE AND A HEDGE IS A CLAIM.**

**What §C8 DID protect is the per-family structure, which was never computed
under C and is not computed here until the hash exists. So E is a confirmatory
test on the arm that matters and an honest re-look on the arm that does not —
and this spec says which is which rather than claiming both.**

## §E2 THE HYPOTHESIS, AND ITS SCOPE IS NOT C's

    H2-GAP   at sites of MODERATE movement, risers are less valence-extreme
             than fallers, beyond the arousal confound.
             A_|valence| (residualised) > 0.   One-sided.

**THIS IS NOT A LARGER REPLICATION OF C's H2. C confirmed H2 on the DISPLACING
stratum — sites of strong movement. The gap is by definition INTERMEDIATE, so
this tests whether the effect holds where movement is moderate**, which is a
different claim about a different population.

**And it is worth running rather than assuming precisely because C's
exploratory gap print reported the gap effect LARGER than the displacing one
(+0.0340 against +0.0251) — a number §C9 forbade reading.** This registration
is how that number becomes readable.

**E DOES NOT MERELY TEST A NEW POPULATION. IT TESTS THE QUALIFIER C's FINDING
IS ALREADY BEING WRITTEN WITH.** The form in circulation — *"a property of
movement, not of displacement sites"* — was adopted BECAUSE the forbidden gap
print was larger, and has been withdrawn on that ground. **So a null here does
not mean "one more stratum came back empty"; it means the wording was wrong.**

**AND THE QUANTITATIVE CASE, MEASURED AND ESTIMATOR-INDEPENDENT: the gap
stratum recovers family signs at 0.884 against the displacing stratum's 0.788,
at every prompt ICC tested.** That is the 5.6x cell count doing what it should.
**E's blind arm is materially better powered than the arm that produced the
contested 26-of-37 on the displacing stratum.**

## §E4 THE FAMILY-GENERALITY ARM — PROMPT-ADJUSTED, NO FREE PARAMETER

**CO-PRIMARY, and neither is named primary because the data do not support a
difference between them:**

    NAIVE      family means over each family's own qualifying cells
    ADJUSTED   fit `A ~ family + prompt` over all qualifying cells, read the
               family term

**WHY THEY ARE CO-PRIMARY AND WHY AGREEMENT IS EXPECTED — stated here so that
agreement is NOT later read as corroboration.** A positive control measured the
adjustment's value at the MEASURED prompt ICC (0.14): sign recovery moves
**0.1–0.3 percentage points** (displacing 0.788 -> 0.791; gap 0.884 -> 0.884).
It begins to pay only at ICC 0.5, which is 3.5x anything measured here. The
arithmetic: prompt SD 0.16 against within-cell noise SD 0.40 — **a sixth of the
variance the family mean already averages over.** The adjustment is principled
and immaterial. **TWO ESTIMATORS THAT DIFFER BY 0.3 POINTS ARE ONE ESTIMATOR
COUNTED TWICE; their agreeing is not evidence.**

**DISAGREEMENT IS THE INFORMATIVE OUTCOME, pre-declared: at these parameters
they should not disagree, so if they do, the fault is in a modelling assumption
and not in the finding.**

**AND THE IDENTIFIABILITY WORRY IS DISCHARGED, not merely unmentioned:** the
same control expected near-disjoint incidence to break estimation. **It does
not — the estimator estimates fine.** The remedy is sound and unnecessary at our
parameters, which is a different verdict from "the remedy fails."

**WHY BOTH, AND NOT A THRESHOLDED CORE:** exactly ONE prompt is common to all 37
families and the Jaccard is *worse* on the gap than on the displacing stratum
(0.61 against 0.68). The thresholded arm's swing on the displacing data — p
0.0015 at k=30, 0.43 at k=34 — **is NOT confounding: prompt-ICC confounding is
far too small to produce it. It is DISCARDING** — 41 prompts survive at k=30,
15 at k=34, 2 at k=36. **A power artefact wearing a robustness label.** So the
full-data estimators are preferred **because they RETAIN EVERY CELL, not because
any adjustment corrects anything** — discarding 96% of prompts is bad at any ICC.

**SECONDARY:** the thresholded core at **k = 20 and k = 30 families**, both
declared here, retained because a reader will ask for it and because its
divergence from the full-data arms is diagnostic of discarding.

    AGREEMENT RULE, declared: agreement is on SIGN ALONE. All p values are
    reported and none is matched against a level — a significance-matching
    rule would reintroduce an undeclared alpha one line below the place this
    section removed a free parameter. If the arms agree in sign, the scope
    claim is stated plainly; if not, ALL are reported and the claim is stated
    as ESTIMATOR-DEPENDENT. No arm is dropped after the fact.

**FAMILY COUNTS DECLARE THEIR PARTITION.** Any "k of N families" cites
`data/lineage_map.json` (`scripts/lineage_map.py`) and names the setting —
`sizes_separate`, `siblings_merged`, or `siblings+predecessors`. **The
principled setting is `siblings_merged`** (size variants of one release are one
recipe; the registry's own edge types distinguish `smaller_sibling_of` from
`smaller_predecessor_of`). The map is CITED, never re-derived.

## §E7 WHAT HAS BEEN SEEN, DECLARED

**Outcome-blind only:** the gap's cell counts, prompt counts, family counts and
coverage geometry ([1805]).

**TWO EXCEPTIONS, DECLARED BECAUSE THEY EXIST AND NOT BECAUSE THEY ARE
COMFORTABLE:**

**(i) VALENCE.** Registration C's §C8 exploratory print reported the gap's
residualised A_|valence| at **+0.0340** with p_up 0.0010, and both seats and the
pen have read it. **This registration is therefore CONFIRMATORY IN DESIGN AND
NOT-BLIND IN FACT on the pooled quantity.**

**(ii) DOMINANCE.** Registration C's H3 verdict recorded that dominance **fired
ONLY in the gap** — read by both seats and the pen. **This is not dismissible as
a different dimension: valence and dominance are computed ON THE SAME CELLS by
THE SAME machinery — same membership null, same residualisation order, same
benchmark construction — so a gap-stratum peculiarity that inflates A for one
dimension is a property of the STRATUM, not of the dimension.** Knowing the gap
was the only place H3 fired is information about how this stratum behaves under
this statistic, and it is declared for that reason.

**Consequences, ruled here rather than discovered later:**

- **The POOLED arm is labelled CONFIRMATORY-BUT-SIGHTED.** Its direction and
  approximate magnitude were known before this spec. It is not a blind test and
  must never be described as one.
- **The FAMILY-GENERALITY ARM (§E4) IS GENUINELY BLIND** — no per-family
  decomposition of the gap has ever been computed, by anyone, and that is the
  arm this registration exists for.
- **Nothing further is read until the hash exists.** The sighted quantity is
  bounded at what §C8 printed; every other quantity here is unseen.

## §E8 LIMITATIONS, IN THE LIMITATIONS

**Prompt-adjustment fixes the ESTIMATOR, not the design.** Families are observed
on largely disjoint item sets — one prompt common to all 37 — and no substrate
in this project fixes that, because it is driven by the per-role rating bar
meeting each model's own vocabulary and it does NOT dilute with pool size (it is
worse on the 5.6x larger gap than on the displacing stratum). **The adjustment
removes the confound from the number; it does not make the roster a balanced
design.**

**The roster's independence is undefended.** Family counts here cite a published
map, but the map is an UPPER BOUND on independence: the registry's relations are
unevenly populated, so genuinely related models with no recorded edge stay
separate. **A more complete registry yields fewer lineages and a larger p, never
the reverse.**

**And H2's own scope, as it stands: confirmed pooled on C's displacing stratum;
its cross-lineage generality NOT distinguishable from chance at the principled
count (16 of 25, p = 0.11).** This registration tests a different population and
a genuinely blind scope arm; **it does not re-open the pooled verdict and cannot
rescue the lineage claim, which is a fact about families and not about strata.**
