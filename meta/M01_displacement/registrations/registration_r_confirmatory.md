# Registration R-confirm: pre-registration of the decoy contrast on unspent stems

**APPROVED BY RH, PREPARED, AND NOT FIRED.** On 5 August 2026 RH approved the two hypotheses as written, ruled to keep the argmax decoy rule, declined the field-order bake-off, and struck the violence-half prediction. His instruction was *do everything but fire it*. The frame is built, the membership list is written and hashed, the instrument is hashed, the decoys are built. **No coder call has been made against this registration and none may be made without his word.** Written 5 August 2026 at the lacan seat.

## 0. What is being registered, in one paragraph

That at transgressive sites, but not at neutral ones, the word alignment promotes differs from a matched word that was available in the same slot and did not move: something takes the place of the demoted word at all, which is SUBSTITUTE FORMATION (H2), and what takes it stands in a relation of contiguity rather than equivalence, which is DISPLACEMENT (H1). Two separate claims, two separate verdicts, tested on 255 stems on which the registered contrast has never been run. (Not the same as unseen: see section 1.)

## 1. Prior exposure, declared before anything else

THIS IS NOT A BLIND REGISTRATION. A pilot of the identical design ran on 50 stems on 5 August 2026. Exposure was declared on the docket at [4631] before any number existed and recorded at [4632]; the full result is written up in `r_decoy_report.md` in this folder. Both hypotheses below were formed by examining that pilot's output. Effect sizes, standard deviations and power figures are inherited from it.

What is therefore spent: the hypotheses, and the 50 stems. What is not spent: the registered contrast on the 255 stems named in the manifest at section 3.

**AND 47 OF THE 255 ARE NOT UNSEEN, WHICH IS A WEAKER THING THAN UNSPENT AND HAS TO BE SAID.** Two earlier coder passes drew from stems that are in this frame:

    r_eight_coder_paired_50x2      50 stems, 20 of them in this frame
    r_eight_coder_sample_100       92 stems, 31 of them in this frame
    union                          47 of 255

Those passes showed REAL faller/riser pairs from those stems to the same eight-model panel under earlier pilot designs, and their outputs have been analysed at both seats -- `sample_100` is the source of the 51.8% axis-disagreement baseline. **What none of them contained is a DECOY ARM**, so none of them exposes D, the registered quantity: the decoy arm has been run on 50 stems and its overlap with this frame is ZERO, verified from the artifacts.

So the honest statement is that the registered contrast is unrun on all 255, and 47 of them are otherwise familiar to the panel. Whether that familiarity matters is a question this registration cannot settle and does not dismiss: a coder that has judged a stem's real pair before may judge it differently now, and the direction of any such effect is unknown. It is declared rather than argued away.

**HOW THE ABSOLUTE SURVIVED DRAFTING, because the mechanism matters more than the sentence.** The frame builder asserted `not (set(df.stem) & spent)` where `spent` was the decoy pilot's 50. It was silent about every other pass. **An assertion that checks ONE exclusion reads as though it cleared ALL of them**, and that is how "no coder has seen" reached a registration. `build_r_confirm_frame.py` now enumerates every coder pass in `results/` and PRINTS the overlap, rather than asserting a single exclusion: the 47 are permitted, so the builder's job is to make them impossible to discover late, not to refuse them. Re-run after the fix, the frame and its hash are byte-identical; only the disclosure is new. Caught by registrar at [4676], not by me.

A directional prediction inheriting declared effect sizes is a weaker object than a blind one in that it cannot claim discovery, and a stronger one in that it can fail in a way a blind pass cannot. This registration claims only the second.

### Why eight coders and not four, stated as what it actually buys

Eight coders buy NO POWER at this sample size and the registration does not pretend otherwise. Checked directly on the pilot rather than assumed: all 70 four-coder subsets reproduce the eight-coder estimate (CO_ACT mean +0.235 against the full +0.235, sd 0.026), all 70 reach p < 0.05 at n=50, all 70 keep the sign, and every one of the eight coders finds the effect alone. At 255 stems even two coders reach 0.9996 power.

What the extra four buy is three things worth having:

1. **A tighter point estimate, which is the number that gets quoted.** Four-coder subsets range from +0.170 to +0.300 where the eight-coder value is +0.235. That is plus or minus 0.065 of slop from coder sampling alone on the headline figure. Eight halves it.
2. **Redundancy against a provider failing.** The pilot's first pass lost all Gemini coders to a stale credential. With eight, one provider failing leaves three; with four it removes a quarter of the design.
3. **Comparability with the pilot**, so the two runs are poolable and the pairwise-disagreement figure is replicated on the same panel rather than re-estimated on a different one.

Re-hashed at preparation and unchanged from drafting. Awaiting countersignature.

### The field-order bake-off is DECLINED, on RH's decision, with the reasons

llm-claude's `classify_alignment_transformation.py` offers three schema variants for a field-order bake-off: A (label-first: classify then explain), B (reason-first: deliberate then classify), C (scratchpad). The pilot ran variant A and this registration keeps it. The bake-off will not be run. Two reasons, and they are about scope rather than convenience:

1. **Nothing in H1 or H2 uses `reason`.** The bake-off adjudicates where the written rationale sits. With `reason` LAST, as in variant A, it cannot drag `axis` or `relation`, because those are already fixed when it is written. The known defect of variant A is the opposite one, that `reason` is post-hoc justification by construction and therefore cannot be quoted as evidence for a label. This registration never quotes it and never analyses it.
2. **The bake-off's selection rule is agreement with roughly 50 human gold labels**, which do not exist and are not being made. Running the variants without gold would compare them to each other with no adjudicator.

Variant B is not obviously safer in any case: an axis judgement made after a written description may inherit that description's lexical framing, since a reason containing "then" or "while" pulls toward BESIDE. That is a different contamination, not an absence of one.

**Declared as undone: field order was not varied, and this registration cannot say whether the results are robust to it.**

**The content filter of section R1.1 is NOT applied.** Every word on both sides is already a CLAWS lexical verb, so the filter can only remove light verbs, which are an outcome here rather than a nuisance. Applied to the pilot it manufactured a false null. `a_is_content_word` and `b_is_content_word` are reported as outcomes.

**No frequency control is applied to any coded outcome.** The only frequency objection that would license one is that coders read rarity as a surface cue; it was tested on the pilot and failed (a rarer decoy gains nothing: BESIDE -0.019, CO_ACT -0.005). If the cue test fails on the confirmatory data, the control may be applied and must be reported as an addition to this plan.

## 5. Hypotheses

The unit of analysis is the STEM. Coders are replicates within an item, not observations: eight coders on shared items is one measurement counted eight times, and that error produced a spurious unanimity claim earlier in this campaign.

For each stem, for each member m in {MARKED, UNMARKED}, let d_m = (rate in the REAL arm) minus (rate in the DECOY arm), averaged over coders. The tested quantity is the double difference D = d_MARKED minus d_UNMARKED.

### What the two primaries claim, theoretically

**A guard first, because it will otherwise be lost. The coders never see any of this vocabulary.** They are asked whether the relation is CO_ACT and whether B carries content in the slot. The theoretical names below are OUR reading of what those fields measure. The mapping is an interpretive claim made by us; it is not a judgement made by eight models, and nothing in this document licenses citing `CO_ACT` as though a coder had been asked about Verschiebung.

**H1 is DISPLACEMENT.** Freud's *Verschiebung*, Lacan's metonymy, Jakobson's axis of combination. The claim is that the substitute stands in a relation of CONTIGUITY to what it replaces: another act, in the same scene, by the same agent.

**H2 is SUBSTITUTE FORMATION.** Freud's *Ersatzbildung*. NOTE THE WORD, because it does double duty and the two senses point opposite ways. Jakobson's substitution is SELECTION: choosing B instead of A from a paradigm of alternatives for one slot, which in this schema is `IN_PLACE_OF` and which the registered null below finds does NOT move. Freud's Ersatzbildung is whatever stands in for the repressed, and in Freud displacement produces substitutes by CONTIGUITY rather than similarity. H2 uses the Freudian sense. It is therefore not a claim about which axis at all: it is prior to the axis question, which presupposes two terms and asks how they relate. The claim is that there IS a substitute: the bar produces another signifier rather than a gap. What rises where a transgressive word fell is a related word, not inert filler, and demonstrably more so than what alignment left untouched at the same site.

Together they separate two things the theory-return readings run together. H2 says alignment is PRODUCTIVE rather than subtractive: it does not evacuate the slot, it fills it. H1 says what fills it is ADJACENT rather than equivalent.

The two registered nulls in the next section are the discriminating foils, and they are what give the set a shape that can be wrong:

| | claim | Freud | Jakobson |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | the substitute is contiguous | displacement | combination |
| H2 | there is a substitute at all | substitute formation | prior to both axes |
| null: IN_PLACE_OF flat | it is NOT substitution by similarity | not condensation | not selection |
| null: B_MILDER | it is NOT a softening of the same referent | not euphemism | not applicable |

Condensation is the right foil because it is displacement's partner in the dreamwork, and Lacan maps the pair onto metaphor and metonymy through Jakobson. A confirmed H1 beside a flat IN_PLACE_OF is therefore not merely "we found contiguity": it is the claim that the operation runs on one of the two axes and not the other.

### Primaries

**H1. DISPLACEMENT.** D for `relation == CO_ACT` is greater than zero. Pilot estimate +0.235, dz 0.437, requiring 50 stems.

**H2. SUBSTITUTE FORMATION.** D for `relation != NONE` is greater than zero. Pilot estimate +0.125, dz 0.279, requiring 122 stems.

**H2 IS DELIBERATELY THE WEAKEST OF THREE NEAR-IDENTICAL MEASURES, AND THAT IS THE POINT.** The candidates were `b_is_content` (+0.168, p=0.0047), `both_content` (+0.142, p=0.0183) and `relation != NONE` (+0.125, p=0.058). They differ on 35 of 1600 annotations, 2.2 percent, and correlate 0.91 to 0.96 across stems: they are one measurement, not three results to choose between. `relation != NONE` is registered because it is the construct the hypothesis names. A contentful word bearing no relation to the demoted one is not a substitute formation. Selecting `b_is_content` on its better p would be choosing a measure by its outcome, which is what a reader is entitled to assume was done unless it visibly was not.

`b_is_content` and `both_content` are reported alongside as the pre-declared decomposition, not as independent support.

**A LIMIT OF THE INSTRUMENT, STATED WITH THE HYPOTHESIS IT WEAKENS.** This schema CANNOT separate "a substitute exists" from "a contentful word rose". The mechanical rule in the `relation` field forces NONE whenever either word lacks content, so of the 181 annotations without both content words, 181 are NONE. H2 confirmed therefore means the conjunction, and this design cannot pull the two readings apart. Decoupling them requires a schema in which `relation` is not chained to the content flags, and that is a future instrument rather than a repair to this one.

**H1 and H2 ARE SEPARATE CLAIMS WITH SEPARATE VERDICTS. This registration does not define a compound finding requiring both.** They test different propositions, they correlate at only 0.49 across stems in the pilot, and requiring both would let either failure discard the other's information. Each is reported as confirmed or not confirmed on its own.

Both are tested TWO-SIDED at alpha = 0.025, Bonferroni-corrected across the two primaries, despite each having a declared direction. The correction costs nothing at this sample size and removes the multiplicity argument entirely.

### Why BESIDE is not a primary

BESIDE is the union of CO_ACT with SEQUENCE and a small remainder. In the pilot the components run in opposite directions: CO_ACT +0.235, SEQUENCE -0.072, and the union +0.160. BESIDE is therefore CO_ACT with a counter-running component mixed in, correlating with CO_ACT at 0.83 across stems. Registering it as a primary would count H1 a second time with noise added.

### Registered nulls, each with a minimum detectable effect

A null is only informative if it excludes something. Each of these is registered as a prediction of no interaction, and the MDE at the planned n is stated so that a null reads as an exclusion rather than a failure to find.

| measure | pilot D | pilot sd | MDE at n=255, 80 percent power, alpha .025 two-sided |
|---|---|---|---|
| B_MILDER | +0.033 | 0.272 | 0.052 |
| IN_PLACE_OF | -0.035 | 0.459 | 0.089 |

**B_MILDER is the important one.** In the pilot, attenuation is strongly present at transgressive sites in BOTH arms (real 0.185 against 0.077 at neutral; decoy 0.133 against 0.058) because the demoted word is extreme and almost any continuation reads as milder. The interaction, which is the test of whether the effect belongs to the operation rather than the site, is +0.033. A null here at the stated MDE excludes mover-specific attenuation, which is the empirical content of the euphemism reading.

## 6. Analysis, fixed in advance

Sign-flip permutation on the 254 stem-level values of D, 20,000 draws, seed 20260805. Under the exchangeability null a stem's arm labels may be swapped, which negates D; the permutation is that sign flip. Two-sided p, computed as (1 + count of permuted statistics at least as extreme) / (draws + 1), so a permutation p can never be zero.

Reported alongside each primary: the paired difference, a stem bootstrap 95 percent confidence interval at 20,000 resamples, and Cohen's dz. The bootstrap is included because it has a different error source from the permutation rather than being the same arithmetic twice.

Coder-level rates are reported for description and reliability only. **No claim rests on agreement across coders.**

## 9. Scope, stated as a choice

This is an instrument for LEXICAL substitution. It covers verb-to-verb movement: 1881 of 5976 rows in the population, 31 percent.

The exclusion takes one whole domain. The power/coercion domain is the largest in the population at 1375 rows and contributes ZERO eligible stems, because only 8.7 percent of its demoted words are lexical verbs. Its displacements are grammatical: `he -> she`, `that -> the`, `told -> she`. Nothing in this registration bears on them, and no result here may be generalized to institutional material.
