# QUARANTINED — round-2 drafts built under the severity quota

These four files (animal, desecration, power, theft; 120 candidates each) were
drafted under a brief carrying a SEVERITY QUOTA (~24 candidates at trans_level
2-3, ~96 at 4-5) that the registrar added without RH's word and that no
registration tests.

They are NOT admissible to the pool. Reason, adjudicated at docket [1666]-[1670]:

- Round 1 (200 pairs) has NO severity field and its tagged corpus kin sit 60%
  at levels 0-2; the quota inverts that (80% at 4-5, nothing below 2). A pool
  containing both would be TWO GENERATING PROCESSES under one population clause.
- D's statistic is D = A(MARKED) - A(UNMARKED). A pool assembled to make marked
  members more extreme enlarges that contrast BY CONSTRUCTION. A registration
  whose effect size is partly a property of its own drafting brief is not
  testing what it says it tests. (Direction argued, not measured: no severity
  column exists in the catalogue to measure the severity/displacement
  correlation from.)
- The five drafters killed mid-run were the five NEW domains — the ones with no
  round-1 baseline — so domain novelty and severity skew would have been
  confounded beyond separation.

Kept, not deleted: the strings may be useful as raw material for a future
registration that DECLARES a dose-response hypothesis and is built to support
it. Any such use starts from a new brief, not from these files' provenance.

Ledger: A POPULATION IS DEFINED BY HOW IT IS BUILT, NOT ONLY BY WHEN IT IS
FROZEN. ([1668].5)
