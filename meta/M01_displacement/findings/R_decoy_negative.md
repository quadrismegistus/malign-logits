# Findings R: the decoy programme — a negative with a cause

Written 2026-08-07 by the registrar seat on RH's commission (write-up push). Sources:
`registrations/registration_r_confirmatory.md` (body `09411ad2adc4df4e`, countersigned
2026-08-05), `registration_r_motivation.md`, the pilot report in the TheoryMachines hub
(`notes/r_decoy/`), `result_r_confirm.json`, `r_confirm_freetext.parquet`, REGISTRATIONS.md row R,
and the docket record of 2026-08-05/06. R is the campaign's fully-registered failure, kept
because the diagnosis is worth more than most confirmations.

## What R asked

Re-code the displacement relation under a redesigned schema, with the axis ASKED rather than
derived, against decoy controls: does the substitute stand in a nameable relation to the demoted
word, beyond what a matched control word would show?

## What happened

**Both primaries failed, with a mechanical diagnosis.** The pilot's +0.235 (largest of ten tests
at n=50) did not survive held-out stems (D −0.012). The cause was found in the coders' own free
text (`r_confirm_freetext.parquet`), not inferred: **every matched control population carried its
own lexical character, and the character was the effect.** The argmax decoy arm over-drew light
verbs (35.0% of picks against 24.2% of the pool); the schema forced `relation = NONE` on them; the
pilot's signal was coders rejecting stranded verbs, not judging a relation. Three control
constructions were tried; three failed; one shared cause.

**The decoy programme is CLOSED as a negative with a cause.** Not "displacement has no relation
structure" — the annotation route that replaced it (Registration S, order-reversal, the identical
word pair as its own control) produced the campaign's strongest positive characterisation of the
relation. R's negative is about CONTROLS: a stationary word is not neutral. Tolerance is a status,
not an absence — a word the base ranked and alignment declined to touch is "endorsed-by-both,"
and faller-vs-decoy is a different contrast wearing a control's label. That sentence, RH's,
later removed decoys from the wave-2/3 design in favour of the within-word control (the same word
demoted in one cell and promoted in another), which holds lexical identity by construction.

## What R bought

- The design lesson above, which repriced every later control.
- The two-stage discipline (pilot with declared exposure, confirmatory held on RH's fire) that S
  inherited and executed.
- The count-your-tests rule: R's +0.235 was the largest of ten tests and did not survive; naming
  the number of tests is the apparatus that made that visible.

## Status

CLOSED. The confirmatory fired once, failed as described, and is not to be re-run: the failure is
diagnosed, the population that produced it is disqualified for the purpose, and the question R
asked was answered in different terms by S (see `findings/S_annotation.md`).
